🏰 Arch Network — Rules

By joining Arch Network, you agree to follow these rules. Breaking them may result in punishment — ignorance is not an excuse.

📜 1. General Conduct

  • Treat all players and staff with respect
  • Harassment, hate speech, racism, or discrimination are strictly forbidden
  • Avoid excessive toxicity, spam, or caps
  • Public chats must remain in English
  • Usernames, skins, builds, and signs must be appropriate

🛑 2. Cheating & Exploits

  • No hacked clients, cheats, macros, or scripts
  • Prohibited: X-Ray, KillAura, AutoClickers, duping
  • Do not exploit bugs or unintended mechanics
  • Report bugs/exploits to staff immediately

Allowed Mods: OptiFine / Sodium, Cosmetic mods, Minimap (no player/entity radar)

⚔️ 3. PvP & Gameplay

  • Combat logging is not allowed
  • Do not abuse safe zones or spawn protection
  • Teaming is prohibited where disallowed
  • Respect claim and protection systems

(Additional gamemode-specific rules may apply)

💬 4. Chat Rules

  • No advertising other servers or Discords
  • Do not impersonate staff or other players
  • Do not bypass the chat filter
  • False reports or staff harassment are forbidden

🧱 5. Builds & Griefing

  • Griefing in protected areas is prohibited
  • No lag machines or lag-inducing builds
  • Offensive or inappropriate builds are not allowed
  • Do not build near others without permission

💰 6. Economy & Trading

  • Scamming or fraud is forbidden
  • Do not exploit shops, crates, quests, or rewards
  • Chargebacks or refund abuse are prohibited
  • No real-world trading for in-game items

⛓ 7. OP Prison Rules

  • Do not abuse OP enchants or mechanics
  • AFK grinding or automated farms are not allowed
  • Exploiting rank, prestige, or perk systems is forbidden

❤️ 8. Lifesteal Rules

  • No heart farming with alternate accounts
  • Stat or kill boosting is prohibited
  • Do not exploit Lifesteal mechanics
  • PvP is enabled — play fair

👥 9. Alt Accounts

  • Alternate accounts are allowed unless abused
  • No alt abuse for rewards, voting, PvP, or Lifesteal
  • Staff may limit alt usage at any time
⚖️ Rules may change at any time.
✨ Play fair, respect others, and enjoy the adventure!
