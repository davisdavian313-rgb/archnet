✨ About Arch Network

Arch Network offers unique gamemodes designed for fun, challenge, and community. Here’s what makes each special:

⛓ OP Prison

Break free from the grind in our OP Prison gamemode. Mine powerful blocks, unlock overpowered enchants, and climb through ranks and prestiges. Strategy and dedication will set you apart as you rise to the top.

🌲 Survival

Classic Minecraft survival with a community twist. Build, explore, and thrive in a world where cooperation and creativity matter. Protect your land with claims, trade with others, and craft your own adventure.

❤️ Lifesteal

A high-stakes PvP experience where every fight matters. Defeat opponents to steal their hearts, but play fair — boosting and exploiting are forbidden. Lifesteal is about skill, risk, and reward in a competitive arena.

🏰 Arch Network — Fair play, creativity, and adventure across every gamemode.
